### 1.0 Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the mandatory cleaning, chemical sanitization, and rotational sporicidal disinfection procedures for ISO Class 5 (Grade A/B) and ISO Class 7 (Grade C) cleanrooms in compliance with FDA 21 CFR Part 211.67 and EU GMP Annex 1.

### 2.0 Scope
This procedure applies to all aseptic manufacturing suites, airlocks, pass-through chambers, and laminar flow hoods located in Building 4 (Facility West).

### 3.0 Regulatory References
- FDA 21 CFR Part 211.67 (Equipment Cleaning and Maintenance)
- EU GMP Annex 1 (Manufacture of Sterile Medicinal Products)
- USP <1072> (Disinfectants and Antiseptics)

### 4.0 Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Cleanroom Operator | Prepare disinfectant solutions, execute sanitization per validated contact times, and log entries contemporaneously. |
| Production Supervisor | Verify sanitization logs, verify rotation schedule, and inspect cleanroom cleanliness prior to batch release. |
| Quality Assurance (QA) | Perform environmental monitoring (EM) sampling, review logbooks, and investigate out-of-specification bioburden. |

### 5.0 Safety & PPE Precautions
- ⚠️ Wear full cleanroom gowning: sterile Tyvek suit, nitrile inner/outer gloves, goggles, and P100 respirator during sporicidal agent application.
- ⚠️ Ensure exhaust ventilation is verified operational prior to handling concentrated quaternary ammonium or peracetic acid compounds.

### 6.0 Approved Disinfectant Rotation Schedule
- **Week 1-3**: Sterile 70% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) filtered through 0.2 µm membrane or Sterile Quaternary Ammonium Solution (Vesphene).
- **Week 4 (Sporicidal Cycle)**: Sterile Hydrogen Peroxide / Peracetic Acid blend (Spor-Klenz) to prevent resistant spore proliferation. Minimum wet contact time: 10 minutes.

### 7.0 Procedural Steps

#### 7.1 Reagent Preparation
**7.1.1 Verify Reagent Expiration** (Cleanroom Operator)
> Inspect disinfectant container lot number, sterile seal integrity, and expiration date. Log lot number in Cleanroom Logbook CL-014.
> - *Critical Parameters*: Lot must be within manufacturer shelf life and sterile filtration date < 28 days.
> - *Acceptance Criteria*: Container seal intact with valid sterility indicator.

#### 7.2 Surface Disinfection
**7.2.1 Unidirectional Overlapping Wiping Technique** (Cleanroom Operator)
> Clean surfaces starting from cleanest area (ISO 5 work zone) towards dirtiest area (return air grilles and exit airlocks). Wipe in overlapping strokes (minimum 20% overlap) in unidirectional parallel paths. Never use circular wiping motions.
> - *Critical Parameters*: Overlap ≥ 20%; Wet contact time ≥ 10.0 minutes for sporicides, ≥ 5.0 minutes for 70% IPA.
> - *Acceptance Criteria*: Surface remains visibly wet for the full duration of the validated contact time.

**7.2.2 Floor Sanitization** (Cleanroom Operator)
> Apply disinfectant using the two-bucket or three-bucket mopping system. Change mop heads every 200 sq ft or whenever entering an adjacent Grade classification zone.

#### 7.3 Environmental Monitoring & Verification
**7.3.1 Post-Cleaning Contact Plate Testing** (QA Specialist)
> QA will take settle plates (TSA with lecithin and polysorbate 80) and RODAC contact plates at designated grid locations within 30 minutes of sanitization completion.
> - *Acceptance Criteria*: Grade A: 0 CFU/plate; Grade B: < 5 CFU/plate; Grade C: < 25 CFU/plate.

### 8.0 Deviation Protocol
If contact time is interrupted or particulate/microbial excursions exceed alert levels, notify the QA On-Call Specialist immediately and initiate a Deviation Report within 24 hours per SOP-QA-002.
