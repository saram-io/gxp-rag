# WI-LAB-007: Gravimetric Verification & Calibration of Single-Channel Micropipettes

## Document Control & Metadata
- **Document ID**: WI-LAB-007
- **Version**: 2.1
- **Department**: Quality Control & Analytical Development
- **Effective Date**: 2024-03-10

---

### 1.0 Purpose
To define the step-by-step gravimetric testing procedure to verify accuracy and precision of single-channel air-displacement micropipettes (0.5 µL to 1000 µL) in compliance with ISO 8655-6 and GLP guidelines.

### 2.0 Equipment & Prerequisites
- Mettler Toledo 5-decimal analytical microbalance (calibrated daily with Class E2 weights).
- Degassed Type I Ultra-pure Water (18.2 MΩ-cm) equilibrated to ambient temperature (20.0°C - 23.0°C).
- Calibrated digital thermometer and hygrometer with valid NIST certificate.

### 3.0 Procedure Steps

#### 3.1 Environmental Equilibration
**3.1.1 Temperature and Relative Humidity Stabilization** (QC Analyst)
> Ensure water, micropipette, and tips have been in the balance testing room for at least 2 hours prior to testing. Record ambient temperature and barometric pressure in the Calibration Logbook.
> - *Critical Parameters*: Room temp 20.0°C - 23.0°C (± 0.5°C during test); Relative humidity 45% - 75%.
> - *Acceptance Criteria*: Environmental conditions within specified tolerances.

#### 3.2 Gravimetric Testing (10 Replicate Measurements)
**3.2.1 Pre-wetting Pipette Tips** (QC Analyst)
> Mount sterile aerosol-barrier pipette tip. Aspirate and dispense nominal test volume 3 consecutive times to saturate vapor pressure inside the tip chamber.

**3.2.2 Gravimetric Dispensing and Mass Recording** (QC Analyst)
> Hold micropipette vertically (90° ± 5°). Immerse tip 2-3 mm into water reservoir. Aspirate slowly, wipe external tip droplet against vessel wall without touching orifice, and dispense into weighing vessel on microbalance. Record mass to 0.00001 g. Repeat for 10 replicate aliquots at 10%, 50%, and 100% nominal volumes.

#### 3.3 Calculations & Acceptance Limits (ISO 8655-6)
**3.3.1 Conversion to Volume using Z-Factor** (QC Analyst)
> Multiply recorded mass by the temperature/pressure-corrected Z-Factor ($V = W \times Z$).
> Calculate Systematic Error (Inaccuracy %E) and Random Error (Imprecision %CV):
> - *1000 µL Pipette*: Inaccuracy %E ≤ ± 0.80%; Imprecision %CV ≤ 0.20%.
> - *100 µL Pipette*: Inaccuracy %E ≤ ± 0.80%; Imprecision %CV ≤ 0.30%.
> - *10 µL Pipette*: Inaccuracy %E ≤ ± 1.20%; Imprecision %CV ≤ 0.80%.

### 4.0 Out-of-Calibration Actions
If any micropipette exceeds ISO 8655 limits, attach a prominent red "OUT OF SERVICE - RECALIBRATION REQUIRED" label, remove from laboratory bench, and submit to the Metrology Department for seal rebuild and recalibration.
