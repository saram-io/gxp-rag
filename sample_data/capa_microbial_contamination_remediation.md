# CAPA-2024-012: Corrective Action & Preventive Action for Bioburden Excursion in Loop-2 Purified Water System

## Document Control & Metadata
- **CAPA ID**: CAPA-2024-012
- **Originating Deviation**: DEV-2024-054
- **Department**: Microbiology & Facilities Engineering
- **Target System**: Distribution Loop-2 USP Purified Water (PW) Generation & Storage
- **Status**: Implementation Phase / Verification of Effectiveness

---

### 1.0 Problem Statement & Investigation Summary
During routine microbiological monitoring on April 8, 2024, sampling point SP-PW-21 (Fill Line 3 point of use) yielded a total aerobic microbial count of 142 CFU/mL (Action limit: 100 CFU/mL, Alert limit: 10 CFU/mL). Species identification revealed *Burkholderia cepacia*.

### 2.0 Root Cause Identification
1. **Biofilm Formation**: Borescopic inspection of distribution pipe section SP-PW-21 revealed internal surface pitting and micro-deadlegs (> 2D pipe diameter) downstream of the manual diaphragm sample valve.
2. **Ozone Sanitization Shadow**: The deadleg geometry prevented ozone dissolved concentration from exceeding the critical 20 ppb sanitization threshold during weekly automatic sanitization cycles.

### 3.0 Corrective Actions Completed
- Re-piped Point-of-Use SP-PW-21 utilizing orbital TIG welding to eliminate deadleg (geometry ratio reduced to < 1.5D) per ISPE Baseline Guide for Water and Steam Systems.
- Performed chemical descaling with 5% citric acid followed by chemical sanitization with 0.5% Peracetic Acid throughout Loop-2.

### 4.0 Preventive Actions & Effectiveness Verification
1. **Ozone Concentration Increase**: Raised automated weekly ozone sanitization setpoint to 50 ppb held for 60 minutes.
2. **Enhanced Monitoring Protocol**: Implemented daily daily microbial testing (R2A agar, 7-day incubation at 30°C-35°C) across all 18 loop sampling points for 30 consecutive calendar days.
3. **Acceptance Criteria for Closure**: Zero CFU/100 mL for *Burkholderia cepacia* and total microbial counts ≤ 5 CFU/mL across all 30 consecutive sampling days.
